Coaching Sevens

How-to guide — using the app at a 7s tournament
Overview Before the day Get the app Score a game Save & upload Review referees Settings & training FAQ Quick reference

1What this app does

The Sevens Coaching Form turns paper referee-coaching sheets into a phone app. Coaches use it pitch-side. It works two ways at a tournament.

For coaches

Score a referee on 7 areas during a game, add quick feedback, then upload the result when you have signal. The form saves on your phone even with no signal at all.

For the organiser

Set up the tournament once — fields, levels, coaches and referees. Share one link or QR code with every coach. After the games, see a ranked table of every referee.

Good to know The app works offline. Forms save to the phone straight away, and upload later when there is signal. Nothing is lost while you wait for bars.

2Before the tournament — organiser setup

Do this once, a few days before the tournament. It saves every coach from typing the same details over and over.

  1. Open the app and go to the Setup tab.
  2. Open Organiser: make a setup.
  3. Fill in the tournament name and a short tournament code.
  4. Add field names, separated by commas (for example 1, 2, 3, 4).
  5. Add the levels being played, one per line (for example Pool play, Cup, Plate).
  6. Add the coaches, one per line, and the referees, one per line.
  7. Tap Make setup link and QR.
  8. Share the link or QR code with every coach before the first game. Print the QR code and pin it up at the coaches' table as a backup.
Tip List every referee at the tournament in the setup, even ones who haven't been assessed yet. The Review tab then shows who still needs games, not just who has already been scored.
Not a password The tournament code is a filter, not a security check. Anyone who has the code can send results in under it. That's fine for a tournament day — just don't treat it as private.

3Getting the app onto each coach's phone

The app installs like a normal app, straight from the browser — there's no app store step.

iPhone / iPad

Open jonobakernz.github.io/auckland-7s-coaching in Safari. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Always open the app from that new icon afterwards — Safari and the icon keep separate, unconnected sets of saved forms.

Android

Open jonobakernz.github.io/auckland-7s-coaching in the browser. Open the browser menu and tap Install app (or use the Install this app button on the Saved tab).

  1. Once installed, open the app from its icon.
  2. Go to Setup and either scan the organiser's QR code, or paste the setup link, then tap Use this setup.
  3. The coach's own name, and the tournament's fields and levels, now appear as ready-made choices on the form.
  4. To double-check what a setup loaded — without exporting anything — open Setup → This device → Referees and coaches.
New coach on the day? They can still use the app without the setup link — they just type the field, tournament and level in by hand on the form.

4Scoring a game

This is what a coach does during and right after each game, on the Form tab.

Match details

  1. Fill in Referee and Coach. If the organiser's setup is loaded, the app suggests referee names and checks the spelling as you type.
  2. Fill in Field and Time.
  3. Open Match details to add the provincial Union hosting the tournament (optional — a dropdown of the 24 NZ provincial unions), the tournament name, date, level, game number, and the referee's role for this game (Referee, Assistant referee 1, or Assistant referee 2).
Saves you re-typing Union, tournament, date and level all carry over automatically to the next form, the same way Start next game carries over the coach and field. You only need to set them once per tournament.

Scoring the 7 areas

Each area is scored from 1 to 5. Tap a number to set it, tap it again to clear it. As you tap, a short line of text confirms what that score means for the level being played — for example, 3 means the referee met the standard for that level, 4 means above it.

The areas are grouped under Auckland Rugby Referees' three key elements to refereeing sevens: decision-making accuracy, referee positioning, and game feel and awareness.

Under each area, quick-tap tags add a common comment (for example Fell behind play Clear whistle). Tap one to drop it straight into the comment box, then edit or add to it as needed. Distance from contest ("too close", "in the way", "good position") is now scored alongside positioning and fitness.

Talking instead of typing

  • Dictation (microphone icon) types what you say into a comment box. It needs signal, since it uses your phone's own speech service. Say "full stop", "comma" or "new line" for punctuation. Māori words and names often come out wrong, so check them afterwards.
  • Voice notes (wave icon) record a short audio clip instead. These stay on the coach's phone only — they are never uploaded and are not included in backup files.

Wrapping up the form

  1. Give the referee an overall Rating score (1 to 5) — this is the number used to rank referees later.
  2. Add one thing the referee did well under Strength to keep.
  3. Add one thing to improve under One thing to work on.
  4. Use Private notes for anything you don't want the referee to see. Private notes are left out of the summary, email and printed copy — only the organiser sees them, and only after the form is uploaded.

5Saving, uploading and sharing

A form saves to the phone automatically as you fill it in — there's no separate save button and nothing is lost if signal drops.

  1. When the form is complete, tap Upload. This sends the result to the organiser's table used for ranking.
  2. No signal yet? That's fine — the form stays saved on the phone, waiting. A yellow number on the Saved tab shows how many forms are waiting to go.
  3. Back in signal? Open the Saved tab and tap Upload all waiting to send every queued form in one go, rather than opening each one.
  4. As soon as the referee's name and rating score are in, tap Start next game. It copies the tournament, coach and level across and adds one to the game number — no upload needed first, so it works with no signal too.
Apply the setup first Uploading before applying the organiser's setup code still works, but the result is uploaded with no tournament code attached. Apply the setup on the Setup tab first, so every result lines up correctly in Review.

Other ways to get a result out

Email a copy

Send the form to any address, including the referee's own.

Share summary

Shares the write-up, including any voice clips and text.

Copy text

Copies a plain-text version to paste anywhere.

Print or save PDF

A printable copy for a referee coordinator or a paper file.

Managing saved forms — the Saved tab

  • See every form saved on this device, and whether each one has uploaded.
  • Select forms to delete clears out old or test forms.
  • Export all as spreadsheet (CSV) — a full local copy of every form on the device.
  • Save backup file and Add forms from backup files — back up a phone before the tournament, or move forms onto another device.

6Reviewing and ranking referees

The Review tab is where the organiser watches results build up through the day, and ranks referees once the tournament is done.

  1. Filter by tournament, role, minimum number of games, and date range.
  2. Tap any column heading to sort the table by it.
  3. Tap a referee's name to read every comment left about them.
How the rank is worked out Referees are ranked by their average overall Rating score. Referees with fewer than 3 games are marked provisional and given no rank yet — 3 games is the target before a rank counts. A tie is split using the average of the 7 area scores.

Adjusting for a hard or easy-marking coach

Tick Adjust ratings for coach marking style to correct for coaches who consistently mark high or low. This only adjusts coaches who have submitted 3 or more forms — the Coach marking table below shows each coach's own average, and how it compares against all coaches.

Referees who still need games

The Needs more assessments list shows any referee below the 3-game target. Referees with no games at all only appear here if they were added to the organiser's setup list — another reason to list every referee up front.

Fixing duplicate spellings

If the same referee ends up listed twice under slightly different spellings, use Names. It spots names that look like the same person and offers to merge them into one.

Getting the ranking out

  • Export ranking table (CSV) — for a spreadsheet, a report, or a referee coordinator.
  • Load results file — if the organiser has a results export from the site's server, load it here to rank every referee at once, rather than relying on live uploads alone.
Exports include the union CSV and JSON exports carry which provincial union hosted the tournament, alongside the tournament, level and game.

7Settings, training and getting help

Display

Choose Light, Dark, or match the phone automatically. There's also a high-contrast Sun mode (black and white, heavy edges) for bright sideline conditions.

Voice dictation

Turn the dictation button on or off for everyone using this device, under Setup → Voice.

Practising before the tournament

Under Setup → Demo and training, tap Load the demo tournament to fill the app with a made-up tournament — 45 games, 12 referees and 4 coaches. Use it to show new coaches how the form and the Review tab work. Demo forms are never uploaded, and a coach's real forms are never affected. Tap Remove the demo data when training is done.

Feedback and support

  • Feedback for the organiser (Setup tab) sends a note straight to the organiser, the same way results upload — it needs signal.
  • Bugs and ideas can go to GitHub (visible to anyone, so leave out real names) or by email instead, for a private note.

8Quick troubleshooting

The form won't upload. Check for signal. The form stays safely saved on the phone either way — upload again later from the Saved tab.

A coach can't see the tournament's fields, levels or coach list. They haven't applied the organiser's setup yet. Go to Setup, scan the QR code or paste the link, then tap Use this setup.

A referee shows no rank, just "provisional". They have fewer than 3 games. Once they reach 3, a rank appears.

Installed the app on an iPhone, but old forms are missing. Safari and the home-screen icon keep separate forms. Always open the app the same way — from the icon, not from Safari.

Dictation is getting names wrong. This is expected, especially for Māori words and names — check and correct dictated text before saving.

Someone uploaded a form before applying the setup code. It still uploads, just with no tournament code attached. Ask them to check Setup, then re-upload if needed.
